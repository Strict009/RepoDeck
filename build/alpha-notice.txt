RepoDeck is a development build.

This is pre-release software. It works, it has been tested, and it is not finished. Things
will change, and some of them will change in ways that are not backwards compatible.

What RepoDeck installs here:

  - The program itself goes into your own user folder. No administrator access is
    required, and none is requested.

What RepoDeck keeps elsewhere:

  - Everything RepoDeck downloads, installs on your behalf, or remembers lives in
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\RepoDeck. That folder is deliberately separate from the program, and
    uninstalling RepoDeck will not delete it. Applications RepoDeck installed for you stay
    where they are.

What RepoDeck will not do:

  - It will not run installers, scripts, or anything else it downloads.
  - It will not ask for administrator access.
  - It will not change Windows settings or touch anything outside its own folders.

RepoDeck cannot tell you whether software you find through it is safe or trustworthy.
Nothing it shows you is a recommendation. It tells you what it found and how it worked it
out, and the decision stays yours.
